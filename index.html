How to read this: The "Best Stock" line shows what would happen if you had a crystal ball and invested your entire portfolio in whichever single S&P 500 stock had the highest return that day — every single trading day. The "Worst Stock" line is the opposite: always picking the single worst performer. This is purely hypothetical and impossible in practice, but it illustrates the extreme range of outcomes hidden inside index returns.

Caveats: Uses current S&P 500 constituents applied historically (survivorship bias). No transaction costs. Dividends are included via adjusted prices. Past performance does not predict future results.
